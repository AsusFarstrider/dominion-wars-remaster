# 1.20 artwork distribution scope

The project owner permits distribution of newly authored and restored project
visuals created by the owner or with ChatGPT. Credit those contributions to the
project owner and contributors; this permission does not transfer rights to
original materials or put all artwork under the code's MIT license. Original game,
Star Trek, and third-party names and artwork remain with their respective owners.

SOURCE-DATA-ALLOWLIST.json identifies exact verified source paths and hashes.
The intermediate Selection and campaign intakes retain earlier authorized
replacement art so their frozen recipes remain usable. Retain only those pinned
forms: DWSSEL2 has identity hashes/classes rather than original reference rasters;
the Briefing bank uses original-reference fingerprints; DWFLEET2 identifies the
player's live palette tables by hash, not literal copies of the original tables.
The updated faction card is the owner's supplied map touch-up converted to RGB565.
The earlier replacement card is retained in predecessor intakes only.

Do not publish the private V1 banks, decoded original rasters or palettes, raw
original files, working exports, or private test evidence. The screenshot gallery
is documentation only, not distributable art source and not part of the player
ZIP. Retain DDrawCompat's BSD-0-Clause attribution and all existing third-party
notices. This is a scoped project distribution record, not blanket legal clearance.
